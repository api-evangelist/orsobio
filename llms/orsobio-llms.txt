## About

OrsoBio takes its name from the metabolic adaptability of bears ("orso" in Italian), which avoid
serious metabolic consequences through hibernation. The company's therapies target fundamental
pathways of energy metabolism. It closed an oversubscribed $67 million Series B in September 2024,
co-led by Ascenta Capital and Woodline Partners, with Samsara BioCapital, Enavate Sciences and
Longitude Capital participating.

## Pipeline

- Mitochondrial protonophores TLC-6740, TLC-1180, TLC-1235 — increase energy expenditure to improve
  metabolic and cardiovascular health in obesity and associated metabolic conditions, and in
  lipodystrophies.
- LXR inverse agonist TLC-2716 — improve plasma triglycerides, cholesterol and hepatic steatosis in
  severe hypertriglyceridemia and MASH.
- ACC2 inhibitor TLC-3595 — improve insulin sensitivity in type 2 diabetes.
- ACMSD inhibitor — augment NAD+ biosynthesis and improve mitochondrial function in metabolic and
  inflammatory liver and/or kidney disorders.
